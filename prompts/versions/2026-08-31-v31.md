## Change from v30

The guide now receives up to six recent exchanges, and source retrieval uses
that bounded conversation context instead of a lexical follow-up classifier.
Short follow-ups retain their topic unless the participant changes it. The
model searches current approved records across the Digital Equity site before
asking one necessary clarification and is explicitly told not to repeat a
clarification loop.

The prompt now states the guide's informational purpose and limits: it can
explain and navigate public site material, but it cannot enroll, book, access
accounts, process requests, decide eligibility, provide case management, or
replace a person. Content marked inactive, outdated, or staging is excluded as
an answer source. No factual response is hardcoded.
