# Digital Equity identity and current-calendar prompt v24

- Source basis: the public Digital Equity site and its current downloadable calendar

This version identifies the Website Guide with the Digital Equity site rather
than treating the broader Fortune Society site as its scope. It retains the
model-first, source-grounded conversation contract from v23.

Calendar questions now receive the current date and a freshly fetched public
downloadable schedule when available. The last known rendered calendar keeps
complete date-to-event rows as a fallback; past rows are never supplied as
upcoming events. The Guide may not invent dates, times, locations, classes, or
availability missing from the selected calendar record.

The exact v23 compiled artifact is preserved in
`2026-08-21-v23-compiled.md`. See `../current.md` for the v24 compiled prompt.
